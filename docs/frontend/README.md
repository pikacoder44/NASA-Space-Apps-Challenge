### Key Principles at a Glance:

1. **The 30-Second Rule:** An exhausted astronaut must understand their overall condition, what changed, and what to do next within 30 seconds.
2. **Plain-Language Communication:** No medical degrees required. Every metric includes its plain name, unit, and a one-sentence explanation of why the user should care.
3. **Dual-Coding for Accessibility:** Never rely on color alone. Every health status combines a color, an icon, and a plain text label (Normal 🟢, Watch 🟡, Act Now 🔴, No Data ⚪).
4. **Orderly Dashboard Hierarchy:** Header summary, mission health banner, 7-vital card grid, multi-day trajectory chart, and daily routine checklist.
5. **Calm, Consistent Design Tokens:** Uses deep space teal (`#176B68`), clear surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), legible text (`#172A2B`), and a 4px baseline grid.
